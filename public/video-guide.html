Install as an App on Any Smartphone

MyWard Bulletin is built with modern Progressive Web App (PWA) technology. Members install it directly from their browser with 1 tap — without needing to search the App Store or Google Play!

🍎

iPhone & iPad (Safari)

Takes only 5 seconds

  1. 1
    Open in Safari

    Tap the bulletin link so it opens in Safari browser.

  2. 2
    Tap the Share Button

    Tap the square icon with an upward arrow 􀈂 Share at the bottom of the screen.

  3. 3
    Tap "Add to Home Screen"

    Scroll down slightly in the share menu and tap ➕ Add to Home Screen.

  4. 4
    Tap "Add" in Top Right

    The MyWard Bulletin icon appears instantly on your home screen! Tap it anytime to view your ward.

✨ Pro-Tip: Once added, it launches in full-screen mode just like a native app from the App Store!
🤖

Android (Chrome & Samsung)

One-tap direct install

  1. 1
    Open in Chrome

    Open the ward bulletin link in Google Chrome.

  2. 2
    Tap "Install MyWard Bulletin"

    A blue banner at the top or bottom of the page will say "Install MyWard Bulletin".

  3. 3
    Or use the 3-Dot Menu (⋮)

    If the button doesn't appear, tap the 3 dots in the top right and select "Install app" or "Add to Home screen".

  4. 4
    Confirmation

    Tap Install. The app is added to your app drawer and home screen.

⚡ Offline Ready: The app caches this week's bulletin so members can read it in church even without mobile data!
